# Rooftop solar connection up to 30 kW (revision 1)

## 1. Scope
This procedure covers the grid connection of rooftop photovoltaic systems of up to 30 kW for prosumers.

## 2. Application steps
1. The customer or installer submits form PV-A with a single line diagram.
2. Drin Energy answers within 30 working days.
3. Systems above 10 kW need a grid study before approval.
4. A certified installer builds the system.
5. Drin Energy inspects it and fits a bidirectional meter within 20 working days of the inspection request.
6. A permission to operate is issued after a successful inspection.

## 3. Technical limits
| Capacity | Connection | Protection |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 5 kW | single phase | inverter built-in |
| 5 to 30 kW | three phase | interface protection relay |

## 4. Inverter requirements
The inverter must comply with EN 50438. Anti-islanding protection must trip within 2 seconds. The power factor must be fixed at 1.0.

## 5. Fees
There is no connection fee for systems up to 5 kW. Larger systems pay 20,000 ALL.
