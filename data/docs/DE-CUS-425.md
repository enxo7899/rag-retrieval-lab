# Moving house and account transfer

## 1. Notice
A customer who moves out must tell Drin Energy at least 5 working days before the move.

## 2. Final reading
A technician or the customer takes a final meter reading on the day of the move. The final bill is issued within 10 working days.

## 3. New tenant or owner
The new occupant opens an account with an identity card and either a property ownership certificate or a signed rental contract. Until the account is open, the previous customer remains responsible for consumption.

## 4. Deceased account holder
If the account holder has died, the account is transferred to the heir within 60 days. The heir presents the death certificate and proof of inheritance. Unpaid bills are not charged to the heir for 60 days.
