# Incident and near-miss reporting

## 1. What must be reported
Every injury, near miss, vehicle accident, fire, spill and equipment damage above 100,000 ALL.

## 2. Deadlines
A fatal or serious injury is reported by phone to the HSE manager immediately and in writing to the labour inspectorate within 24 hours. A lost-time injury is entered in the HSE system within 24 hours. A near miss is entered within 72 hours.

## 3. Investigation
The line manager starts a level 1 investigation within 5 working days. Serious events get a level 2 investigation led by the HSE manager with a root cause analysis within 30 days.

## 4. Contractors
Contractors report to the Drin Energy site supervisor, who enters the event. A contractor who hides an incident may lose site access.

## 5. No blame
Reporting a near miss in good faith is never grounds for discipline.
