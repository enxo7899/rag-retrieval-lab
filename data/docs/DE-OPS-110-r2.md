# Planned outage notification

## 1. Scope
This procedure applies to every planned interruption for maintenance or network works on the low-voltage and medium-voltage networks of Drin Energy.

## 2. Minimum notice periods
| Customer class | Minimum notice | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Households | 5 working days | SMS and website |
| Small business | 5 working days | SMS and email |
| Hospitals and critical care | 15 working days | Phone call and letter |
| Large industrial | 10 working days | Account manager |

## 3. Procedure
1. The planner submits the outage request in GridPlan at least 20 working days before the works.
2. The control room approves the switching programme.
3. The Customer Contact Team sends the notices listed in section 2.
4. A reminder is sent 24 hours before the interruption starts.
5. If the works are postponed, affected customers are told within 2 hours.

## 4. Duration and timing
A planned interruption must not last longer than 8 hours unless a mobile generator has been offered. Works are scheduled between 08:00 and 18:00 on weekdays. No planned interruption takes place on public holidays.

## 5. Priority customers
Customers on the Priority Services Register receive a personal phone call. They are offered a generator loan when the interruption is expected to exceed 4 hours.
