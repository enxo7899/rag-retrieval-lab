# Procurement approval thresholds

## 1. Purpose
Sets how much competition and approval a purchase needs.

## 2. Thresholds
| Purchase value | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Below 500,000 ALL | one written quote |
| 500,000 to 5,000,000 ALL | three written quotes |
| Above 5,000,000 ALL | public tender |
| Above 20,000,000 ALL | public tender and board approval |

## 3. Splitting
Splitting a purchase to stay under a threshold is a breach of this policy and is reported to internal audit.
