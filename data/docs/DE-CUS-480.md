# Net billing for prosumers

## 1. Who is a prosumer
A customer with a rooftop solar system of up to 50 kW connected under the grid connection procedure.

## 2. Settlement
Every month the energy delivered to the grid is credited at the export rate of 6.50 ALL/kWh. The credit is subtracted from the bill for energy taken from the grid.

## 3. Surplus
A surplus credit is carried forward for up to 12 months. After 12 months, any unused credit expires without payment.

## 4. Statement
An annual statement of imports, exports and credits is sent by 31 January.
