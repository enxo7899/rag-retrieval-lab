# Compensation for supply interruptions (revision 1)

## 1. When compensation is paid
Customers receive compensation when an unplanned interruption lasts longer than the limits below.

## 2. Amounts
| Interruption longer than | Household | Business |
|---|---|---|
| 12 hours | 1,000 ALL | 2,000 ALL |
| 24 hours | 2,500 ALL | 5,000 ALL |

## 3. How to claim
Every claim must be made by the customer within 30 days of the interruption.

## 4. Exclusions
No compensation is paid for interruptions caused by storms, floods, earthquakes, the customer's own installation or a disconnection for non-payment.
