# Substation lockout and tagout

## 1. Purpose
To make sure equipment is dead, locked and earthed before anyone works on it.

## 2. Who may isolate
Only an Authorised Person with a valid Grade A switching certificate may isolate equipment. The certificate is issued by the Drin Energy Training Centre and must be renewed every 2 years.

## 3. Isolation steps
1. Obtain a permit to work (form PTW-1) from the control room.
2. Open the isolator and fit your personal red padlock.
3. Attach a danger tag showing your name and the permit number.
4. Prove the circuit dead with an approved voltage detector on all phases. Test the detector on a known live source before and after.
5. Apply earths on each side of the work location.
6. Report to the control room, which confirms that the isolation is complete.

## 4. Removing a lock
Only the person who fitted a lock may remove it. If that person cannot be reached, the Duty Manager may have the lock cut after 3 documented attempts to contact them and a physical check that the site is clear.

## 5. Emergencies
In an emergency, de-energise first and complete the paperwork afterwards. Report the action to the control room immediately.
