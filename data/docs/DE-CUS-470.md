# Customer data protection

## 1. Principles
Drin Energy processes customer personal data only to supply electricity, to bill, to answer requests and to meet legal duties.

## 2. Retention
Billing records are kept for 10 years. Meter consumption data is kept for 5 years. Call recordings are deleted after 12 months.

## 3. Smart meter data
Smart meters send a reading every 15 minutes only if the customer has given consent. Without consent, one daily reading is collected.

## 4. Your rights
A customer can ask for a copy of their data, a correction or deletion where the law allows. Requests are answered within 30 days. Write to the data protection officer at privacy@drinenergy.example.
