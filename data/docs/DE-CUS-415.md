# Late payment, disconnection and reconnection

## 1. Late payment interest
Interest of 0.05 percent per day is charged on the overdue amount from the day after the due date.

## 2. Reminder timeline
1. Day 1 after the due date: reminder by SMS.
2. Day 15: written notice.
3. Day 30: final notice that names the date of disconnection.
4. Disconnection takes place no earlier than 45 days after the due date.

## 3. Fees
| Service | Household | Business |
|---|---|---|
| Disconnection visit | 800 ALL | 1,600 ALL |
| Reconnection within 24 hours of payment | 1,200 ALL | 2,400 ALL |
| Same-day emergency reconnection | 2,500 ALL | 4,500 ALL |

## 4. When supply is never cut
Supply is not disconnected on Friday after 12:00, at weekends or on public holidays. Households on the social tariff are protected from disconnection between 1 December and 28 February.

## 5. Payment plan
A customer in difficulty can pay off the debt in up to 6 monthly instalments after paying 20 percent upfront. While the plan is kept, no disconnection takes place.
