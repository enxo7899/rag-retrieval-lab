# Tariff schedule 2025

## 1. Tariffs
| Tariff | Energy rate | Fixed monthly charge |
|---|---|---|
| Household, first 300 kWh a month | 7.90 ALL/kWh | 160 ALL |
| Household, above 300 kWh a month | 11.40 ALL/kWh | included |
| Household night saver, day 07:00 to 23:00 | 9.60 ALL/kWh | 160 ALL |
| Household night saver, night 23:00 to 07:00 | 5.20 ALL/kWh | included |
| Small business | 11.80 ALL/kWh | 600 ALL |
| Large industrial | 10.20 ALL/kWh | 1,350 ALL per kW of demand |

## 2. Social tariff
Households that receive social assistance pay 5.80 ALL/kWh for the first 150 kWh each month.

## 3. Tax
All rates exclude value added tax. VAT of 20 percent is added to the bill.

## 4. Changes
These tariffs applied from 1 January 2025 until 31 December 2025. Any future change is announced at least 30 days before it takes effect.
