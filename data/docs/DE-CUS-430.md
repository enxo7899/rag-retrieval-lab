# Priority Services Register for vulnerable customers

## 1. Who can register
Customers who rely on medical equipment that needs electricity, people over 75 who live alone, people with a severe disability and people with a chronic illness.

## 2. How to register
Registration needs a certificate from a doctor, except for people over 75. The customer must confirm the registration every year.

## 3. What registered customers receive
A personal phone call before planned interruptions, no disconnection for non-payment between 1 December and 28 February, a generator loan during long interruptions and free relocation of the meter if needed.

## 4. Staff duty
Agents must always ask if anyone in the home depends on medical equipment before agreeing to a disconnection.
