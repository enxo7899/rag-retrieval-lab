# Meter seal replacement (revision 2)

## 1. Purpose and scope
This procedure covers the replacement of a broken, missing or tampered seal on a customer electricity meter. It applies to field technicians of grade T2 and above. It replaces revision 1.

## 2. Before you start
Check the work order in FieldDesk. Carry at least 5 numbered tamper-evident seals of type S-09, sealing pliers and a camera. The customer does not need to be present, but must receive an SMS 2 hours before arrival.

## 3. Procedure
1. Photograph the meter, the existing seal and the seal number before touching anything.
2. Confirm the terminal cover is closed and no bare conductors are visible.
3. Cut the old seal wire with the pliers and keep the old seal in an evidence bag.
4. Thread the new S-09 seal, pull the wire tight and crimp it.
5. Photograph the new seal and record its number in FieldDesk within 4 hours.
6. Leave a seal notice card at the property.

## 4. Suspected tampering
If you see signs of tampering, such as a melted terminal block or bypass wires, do not repair anything. Stop work, photograph the installation and call Revenue Protection on internal line 4410 within 4 hours. Do not accuse the customer on site.

## 5. Records
The seal log and photographs are kept for 5 years. Used seal numbers must never be reused.
