# Prepaid meters and top-up

## 1. Who can use a prepaid meter
Households and small shops without arrears can ask for a prepaid meter. The meter is installed free of charge.

## 2. Topping up
Credit can be bought in the app, at agents and at post offices. The minimum top-up is 500 ALL.

## 3. Emergency credit
When the balance reaches zero, the meter gives 300 ALL of emergency credit. It is deducted from the next top-up. Supply is never cut at night, at weekends or on holidays when the balance is zero.

## 4. Low credit warning
The display warns when the credit will last less than 3 days at the average consumption.

## 5. Changing to a normal bill
A prepaid customer can switch to a monthly bill. The change is made within 15 working days. A faulty prepaid meter is replaced within 2 working days.
