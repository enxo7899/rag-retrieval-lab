# Meter reading and estimated bills

## 1. Reading schedule
Meter readers visit every customer once every 3 months. In the other months the bill is based on a customer reading or an estimate.

## 2. Customer reading
Customers can send their own reading by app or SMS between the 25th and the 28th of each month.

## 3. Estimates
An estimate uses the consumption of the same month in the previous year. If no history exists, the average of the customer class is used.

## 4. Two estimates in a row
After two consecutive estimated bills the meter must be read within 10 working days. The difference is corrected in the next bill, without penalty to the customer.
