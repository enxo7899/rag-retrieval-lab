# Vegetation clearance near overhead lines

## 1. Scope
Tree and brush clearance along overhead line corridors of all voltages.

## 2. Minimum clearances
| Line voltage | Minimum clearance to conductors |
|---|---|
| 0.4 kV insulated cable | 1.0 m |
| 10 kV | 2.5 m |
| 20 kV | 3.0 m |
| 35 kV | 4.0 m |
| 110 kV | 5.5 m |

## 3. Cutting cycle
Urban corridors are cleared every 2 years and rural corridors every 3 years. After a storm, damaged corridors are inspected within 10 days.

## 4. Landowners
Landowners are notified 14 days before work starts. If a landowner refuses access, the case goes to the legal department and the line section is marked as high risk.

## 5. Nesting season
No cutting is done between 15 March and 31 July, except in an emergency where trees touch a conductor.
