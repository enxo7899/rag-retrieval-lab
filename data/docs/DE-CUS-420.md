# Billing disputes and complaints

## 1. How to complain
A customer can complain by phone on 0800 1100, by email, through the app or in person at any customer centre.

## 2. Time limits
Every complaint is acknowledged within 2 working days. A decision is sent within 20 working days, or 40 working days for complex cases that need a site visit.

## 3. Disputed bills
The customer may pay only the undisputed part of the bill. While a dispute is open, no late interest is charged on the disputed amount and the supply is not disconnected.

## 4. Meter accuracy test
A customer may ask for the meter to be tested. The test is free if the meter is found to be outside plus or minus 2 percent. Otherwise the customer pays 3,500 ALL.

## 5. Escalation
If Drin Energy does not answer within the time limit, or the customer disagrees with the decision, the customer can take the case to the energy regulator ERE.
