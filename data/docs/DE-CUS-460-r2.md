# Compensation for supply interruptions (revision 2)

## 1. When compensation is paid
Customers receive compensation when an unplanned interruption lasts longer than the limits below. Planned works that overrun the announced time are treated in the same way.

## 2. Amounts
| Interruption longer than | Household | Business |
|---|---|---|
| 8 hours | 1,500 ALL | 3,000 ALL |
| 12 hours | 3,000 ALL | 6,000 ALL |
| 24 hours | 6,000 ALL | 12,000 ALL |

For every further 12 hours after 24 hours, 1,000 ALL is added.

## 3. How to claim
Compensation for interruptions longer than 24 hours is credited automatically to the next bill within 30 days. Shorter interruptions must be claimed within 60 days.

## 4. Exclusions
No compensation is paid for interruptions caused by storms with winds above 90 km/h, floods, earthquakes, the customer's own installation or a disconnection for non-payment.
