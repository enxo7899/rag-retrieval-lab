# Transformer oil sampling and testing

## 1. Scope
This procedure applies to oil-filled distribution transformers from 10/0.4 kV up to 35/10 kV.

## 2. Sampling frequency
| Transformer rating | Dissolved gas sample | Full oil test |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 630 kVA | not required | every 6 years |
| 631 to 2500 kVA | every year | every 3 years |
| Above 2500 kVA | every 6 months | every year |

## 3. Taking a sample
1. Record the oil temperature and the ambient temperature.
2. Open the sampling valve and purge 1 litre of oil into the waste container.
3. Fill a 1 litre amber glass bottle from the bottom without trapping air bubbles.
4. Label the bottle with the transformer serial number, date and temperature.
5. Deliver the bottle to the laboratory within 48 hours.

## 4. Acceptance limits
| Property | Limit |
|---|---|
| Breakdown voltage | at least 50 kV |
| Water content | at most 20 ppm |
| Acidity | at most 0.15 mg KOH/g |
| Acetylene in dissolved gas | at most 5 ppm |

## 5. Actions on failure
If the breakdown voltage is below 30 kV, the oil must be filtered or replaced within 14 days. Any acetylene above 5 ppm raises an engineering alarm and the transformer is inspected within 7 days.
