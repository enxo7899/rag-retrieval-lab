# Energy efficiency subsidy programme

## 1. Purpose
Household customers can get part of the cost of energy saving measures refunded.

## 2. Eligible measures
| Measure | Share refunded | Maximum refund |
|---|---|---|
| Heat pump | 30 percent | 150,000 ALL |
| Wall and roof insulation | 25 percent | 80,000 ALL |
| Solar water heater | 30 percent | 60,000 ALL |

## 3. How to apply
1. Apply on the website before buying anything.
2. Wait for the approval letter, which arrives within 15 working days.
3. Buy and install the equipment with a certified installer.
4. Upload the invoice and installation certificate.

## 4. Payment
The refund is paid within 45 days of the complete documents. Ordinary light bulbs and appliances are not eligible.
