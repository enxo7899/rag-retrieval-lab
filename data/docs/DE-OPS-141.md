# Low voltage fault location and restoration

## 1. Scope
This procedure covers faults reported on the 0.4 kV distribution network, including no-supply calls and voltage complaints.

## 2. Response targets
| Priority | Situation | Attend within |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Danger to life or fire | 1 hour |
| P2 | Loss of supply to more than 50 customers | 3 hours |
| P3 | Loss of supply to a single customer | 24 hours |
| P4 | Flickering lights or low voltage | 3 working days |

## 3. Procedure
1. Log the call in the outage management system and ask whether the neighbours also have no supply.
2. Ask the customer to check their own main fuse or breaker.
3. Check the service fuse at the pole or cabinet.
4. Locate the fault by sectionalising the feeder and testing each section.
5. Repair or isolate the fault and restore supply, then close the call with the cause code.

## 4. Temporary supply
If the repair will take more than 6 hours, connect a mobile generator to restore supply to the affected customers.

## 5. Escalation
If a P2 fault is still open after 5 hours, the duty engineer informs the operations director and the Customer Contact Team publishes an update every 2 hours.
