# Password and system access policy

## 1. Passwords
Passwords have at least 14 characters. They are changed every 180 days or after a suspected leak.

## 2. Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication is required for the control room network, email and every remote access.

## 3. Lockout
An account is locked for 15 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts.

## 4. Vendors
Vendors reach the operational network only through the jump host. Their sessions are recorded. Shared accounts are not allowed.
