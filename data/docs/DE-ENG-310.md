# Low voltage cable sizing

## 1. Scope
Standard aluminium four-core cables for the 0.4 kV network.

## 2. Current ratings
| Cross-section | Continuous current | Maximum length for 3% voltage drop |
|---|---|---|
| 16 mm2 | 80 A | 95 m |
| 25 mm2 | 105 A | 120 m |
| 35 mm2 | 125 A | 140 m |
| 50 mm2 | 150 A | 160 m |
| 70 mm2 | 185 A | 190 m |
| 95 mm2 | 225 A | 220 m |
| 150 mm2 | 290 A | 270 m |

## 3. Derating
Ratings assume a ground temperature of 25 degrees C. Multiply by 0.93 at 30 degrees C and by 0.85 at 35 degrees C. Cables laid in ducts are derated by a further 0.9.

## 4. Voltage drop
The total voltage drop from the transformer to the customer terminals must not exceed 5%.
