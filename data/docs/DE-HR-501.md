# Field technician on-call policy

## 1. Rota
Each field technician is on call one week in every four. The rota is published 4 weeks ahead.

## 2. Response
The on-call technician acknowledges a call within 10 minutes and must reach the site within 60 minutes in Tirana and 90 minutes elsewhere.

## 3. Allowance
The on-call allowance is 6,500 ALL per week. Time worked on a call out is paid at 1.5 times the normal hourly rate.

## 4. Rest
A technician who works after midnight has at least 11 hours of rest before the next shift.
