# Battery storage connection requirements

## 1. Scope
Battery energy storage systems connected to the distribution network, from 50 kW upwards.

## 2. Technical requirements
The system must have a battery management system that disconnects on over-voltage, under-voltage and over-temperature. The power ramp rate is limited to 10 percent of rated power per minute.

## 3. Siting
Containers must stand at least 3 metres from buildings and have a fire suppression system approved by the fire service. Indoor installation above 20 kWh is not allowed.

## 4. Metering and export
A separate meter records charge and discharge. The export limit agreed in the connection offer is enforced by the plant controller.
