# Billing and payment terms

## 1. Billing cycle
Households and small businesses are billed monthly. The bill is issued by the 5th of the month after the consumption period.

## 2. Due date
A bill is due 20 days after the issue date.

## 3. Payment channels
Bills can be paid in the mobile app, by online banking, at any bank branch, at agents and at post offices. Payment online and at banks is free. Cash payment at a post office carries a fee of 50 ALL.

## 4. Paperless discount
Customers who choose the electronic bill and direct debit receive a discount of 30 ALL on every bill.

## 5. Deposits
A new business customer pays a deposit equal to the average consumption of 2 months. The deposit is returned after 24 months of on-time payments.
