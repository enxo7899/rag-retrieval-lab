# Working at height and protective equipment

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to all employees and contractors who climb poles, towers, ladders or work on roofs.

## 2. Required protective equipment
| Task | Head | Hands | Body |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pole climbing | Class E helmet with chin strap | Insulating gloves class 2 | Harness with two lanyards |
| Ladder work on low voltage | Class E helmet | Insulating gloves class 0 | Ladder belt |
| Tower work | Class E helmet with chin strap | Work gloves | Full body harness and fall arrester |

## 3. General rules
Any work at a height of 2 metres or more requires a harness. Before climbing, every wooden pole is tested with a hammer and checked for rot. A pole older than 30 years may only be climbed after it has been stayed or with an elevated work platform.

## 4. Weather limits
Stop climbing when the wind exceeds 40 km/h, when lightning is seen within 10 km, or when the surface is icy.

## 5. Rescue
A second person trained in pole-top rescue must be present on site. A rescue kit is carried in every line vehicle.
