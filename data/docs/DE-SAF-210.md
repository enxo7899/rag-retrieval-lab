# Arc flash boundaries and protective categories

## 1. Scope
Rules for people working near energised switchgear and panels.

## 2. Boundaries
| Equipment | Voltage | Arc flash boundary | PPE category |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low voltage distribution panel | 0.4 kV | 0.9 m | 2 |
| Medium voltage switchgear | 10 kV | 1.5 m | 3 |
| Medium voltage switchgear | 35 kV | 3.0 m | 4 |

## 3. Rules
Nobody may work live on medium voltage equipment. Live low-voltage work needs a written risk assessment signed by the supervisor and a second person watching. Switching operations on 10 kV and 35 kV equipment are done with the remote operating handle where one is fitted.

## 4. Labels
Every panel carries a label with the boundary distance and the PPE category. Labels are checked every 12 months during the substation inspection.
